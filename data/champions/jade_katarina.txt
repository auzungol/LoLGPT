Katarina, the The Sinister Blade

- Bouncing Blades (Q) deals damage and marks enemies, Katarina can consume the mark with spells or attacks to deal extra magic damage. - Sinister Steel (W) does damage around Katarina and increases her movement speed if the ability hits a champion. - Shunpo (E) deals damage and blinks to target; afterwards the ability provides damage reduction to Katarina.

Role: Assassin, Mage
Region: Unknown
Resource: None

Passive - Voracity: Champion kills or assists reduce Katarina's cooldowns by 15 seconds.

Q - Bouncing Blade: Katarina throws a dagger that bounces from enemy to enemy, dealing magic damage.

W - Sinister Steel: Katarina whirls her daggers around her, dealing magic damage to all enemies in the area. If she hits an enemy Champion, Katarina gains a burst of speed for a short duration.

E - Shunpo: Katarina instantly moves to her target's location and takes reduced damage from enemies for a few seconds. If the target is an enemy, she deals damage.

R - Death Lotus: Katarina becomes a flurry of blades, throwing daggers with unrivaled speed at up to three nearby Champions.
