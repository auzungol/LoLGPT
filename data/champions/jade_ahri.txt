Ahri, the The Nine-Tailed Fox

- Essence Theft (P) is stacked on damaging enemies - Spirit Rush (R) doesn't reset

Role: Mage, Assassin
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Essence Theft: Gains a charge of Essence Theft whenever a spell hits an enemy. Upon reaching maximum charges, the next spell restores Ahri's Health.

Q - Orb of Deception: Ahri sends out and pulls back her orb, dealing magic damage on the way out and true damage on the way back.

W - Fox-Fire: Ahri releases three fox-fires, that lock onto and attack nearby enemies.

E - Charm: Ahri blows a kiss that damages and Charms the first enemy hit. They also take more damage from her for several seconds.

R - Spirit Rush: Ahri dashes forward and fires essence bolts, damaging 3 nearby enemies (prioritizes Champions). Spirit Rush can be cast up to three times before going on cooldown.
