Warwick, the The Blood Hunter

- The Abilities are completely different - Infinite Duress (R) is a point and click suppress

Role: Fighter, Tank
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Eternal Thirst: Each of Warwick's attacks will heal him. Each successive attack against the same target will steal more and more Health.

Q - Hungering Strike: Takes a bite out of an enemy unit and heals Warwick.

W - Hunter's Call: Warwick lets out a howl, increasing all friendly champions' Attack Speed for a short time.

E - Blood Scent: Warwick passively senses weakened enemy champions around him.  The scent of blood sends him into a fury, causing him to move at incredible speeds.

R - Infinite Duress: Warwick lunges at an enemy champion, suppressing his target and dealing magic damage for a few seconds.
