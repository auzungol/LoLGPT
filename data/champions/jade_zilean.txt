Zilean, the The Chronokeeper

- Time Bomb (Q) is point and click - Heightened Learning (P) grants experience to his entire team

Role: Support, Mage
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Heightened Learning: Zilean and his allies gain additional experience.

Q - Time Bomb: Zilean places a time bomb on his target, which damages nearby enemies once it expires.

W - Rewind: Zilean reduces all of his other cooldowns.

E - Time Warp: Zilean increases an allied Champion's movement speed or slows an enemy champion.

R - Chronoshift: Zilean marks himself or an allied champion with a protective time rune. If they would take lethal damage, they are instead transported back in time and healed.
