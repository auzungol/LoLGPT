Gangplank, the The Saltwater Scourge

- Cannon Barrage (R) has random cannonballs instead of consistent AOE damage - Raise Morale (E) grants move speed and Attack damage both passively and upon activation - Parrrley (Q) grants gold (no more silver serpents)

Role: Fighter
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Grog-Soaked Blade: Gangplank's attacks apply a poison that deals magic damage per second, increasing with level.

Q - Parrrley: Gangplank takes aim and shoots an enemy unit with his pistol. If Parrrley deals a killing blow, he gains extra gold and half the mana cost is refunded.

W - Remove Scurvy: Consumes a large quantity of citrus fruit which clears any crowd control effects on him and heals him.

E - Raise Morale: Gangplank fires a shot into the air, increasing nearby allied champions' attack damage and movement speed.

R - Cannon Barrage: Gangplank signals his ship to fire upon an area for 7 seconds, slowing enemies and dealing damage in random locations within the area.
