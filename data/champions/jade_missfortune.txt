Miss Fortune, the The Bounty Hunter

- Strut (P) moved from W - Impure Shots (W) passive deals magic damage on each hit with active increasing attack speed and applying grievous wounds

Role: Marksman, Mage
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Strut: After 5 seconds of not being attacked, Miss Fortune gains an additional 25 Movement Speed. This bonus increases by 9 each second up to a maximum bonus of 70.

Q - Double Up: Miss Fortune fires a bullet at an enemy, damaging them and a target behind them.

W - Impure Shots: Miss Fortune passively increases damage dealt to a target with each strike.  This ability can be activated to increase Miss Fortune's Attack Speed and cause her attacks to lower healing received by the target.

E - Make It Rain: Miss Fortune unleashes a flurry of bullets at a location, dealing waves of damage to opponents and slowing them.

R - Bullet Time: Miss Fortune channels a flurry of bullets into a cone in front of her, dealing large amounts of damage to enemies.
