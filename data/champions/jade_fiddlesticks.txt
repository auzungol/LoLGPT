Fiddlesticks, the The Harbinger of Doom

- The Abilities are completely different, except for the ultimate Crowstorm (R) - Fiddlesticks fear makes the enemy walk randomly

Role: Mage, Support
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Dread: Nearby enemies have reduced Magic Resist.

Q - Terrify: Strikes a target unit with fear, causing it to flee in terror for a duration.

W - Drain: Fiddlesticks saps the life force of an enemy, dealing damage to a target over time and healing himself.

E - Dark Wind: A wisp of wind strikes an enemy unit and then bounces to nearby enemy units, dealing damage and silencing the victims.

R - Crowstorm: A murder of crows flock wildly around Fiddlesticks, dealing damage each second to all enemy units in the area.
