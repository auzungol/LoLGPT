Jax, the Grandmaster at Arms

- Grandmaster's Might (R) grants stacking attack speed per strike - Equipment Mastery (P) grants health for power

Role: Fighter
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Equipment Mastery: Jax's gains bonus Health based on his bonus Attack Damage and Ability Power from items.

Q - Leap Strike: Jax leaps and strikes.

W - Empower: Jax's next attack or Leap Strike deals bonus damage and damages nearby enemies.

E - Counter Strike: Jax enters a defensive stance, dodging all incoming basic attacks and taking less damage from area of effect abilities. Then he stuns surrounding enemies and damages them.

R - Grandmaster's Might: Jax gains attack speed when he strikes. Every third consecutive attack deals bonus damage.
