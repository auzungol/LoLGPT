Ryze, the The Rogue Mage

- The Abilities are completely different (for the 5th time)

Role: Mage
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Arcane Mastery: Ryze's abilities reduce the cooldown of all other abilities.

Q - Overload: Passive: Ryze permanently gains Cooldown Reduction.  Active: Ryze unleashes an orb of energy, dealing magic damage to his target.

W - Rune Prison: Ryze magically imprisons an enemy, rooting them and dealing magic damage.

E - Spell Flux: Ryze fires a chaotic bolt of magic that can bounce between enemies, dealing magic damage and reducing their Magic Resist.

R - Desperate Power: Ryze temporary harnesses his full powers, gaining area of effect damage on all abilities, Spell Vamp, and Move Speed for a short time.
