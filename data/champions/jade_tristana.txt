Tristana, the The Megling Gunner

- Explosive Shot (E) is a DoT ability

Role: Marksman, Assassin
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Draw a Bead: Increases Tristana's attack range as she levels.

Q - Rapid Fire: Tristana fires her weapon rapidly, increasing her attack speed for a short time.

W - Rocket Jump: Tristana fires at the ground to propel herself to a distant location, dealing damage and slowing surrounding units for 3 seconds where she lands.

E - Explosive Shot: When Tristana kills a unit, her cannonballs burst into shrapnel, dealing damage to surrounding enemies.  Can be activated to deal damage to target unit over time, and reduce healing received.

R - Buster Shot: Tristana loads a massive cannonball into her weapon and fires it at an enemy unit. This deals magic damage and knocks the target back.
