Soraka, the The Starchild

- The Abilities are completely different, except for the ultimate Wish (R) - Can solo lane - Has a point and click silence

Role: Support, Mage
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Consecration: Increases surrounding allies' Magic Resist by 16.

Q - Starcall: A shower of stars falls from the sky, striking all nearby enemies for magic damage and reducing their Magic Resist for a short duration.

W - Astral Blessing: Soraka blesses a friendly unit, restoring health and increasing Armor for a short time.

E - Infuse: Restores Mana to an ally, or deals damage to an enemy and silences them for a short duration.

R - Wish: Soraka fills her allies with hope, instantly restoring health to herself and all friendly champions.
