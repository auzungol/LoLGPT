Twisted Fate, the The Card Master

- Gate (E) is a global teleport - Destiny (R) is a global slow and reveal - Loaded Dice (P) grants all allies and himself gold on kill

Role: Mage, Marksman
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Loaded Dice: Twisted Fate and his allies receive an additional 2 gold per kill.

Q - Wild Cards: Twisted Fate throws three cards, dealing damage to each enemy unit they pass through.

W - Pick A Card: Twisted Fate chooses a magic card from his deck, and uses that for his next attack, causing bonus effects.

E - Gate: After channeling, Twisted Fate Teleports to any target location.

R - Destiny: Twisted Fate predicts the fortunes of his foes, revealing and slowing all enemy champions. Gate's channel time is also reduced during this duration.
