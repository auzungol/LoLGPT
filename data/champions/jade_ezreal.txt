Ezreal, the The Prodigal Explorer

- Essence Flux (W) passes through all units dealing damage to enemies and granting allies attack speed - Trueshot Barrage (R) deals less damage per enemy hit

Role: Marksman, Mage
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Rising Spell Force: Ezreal gains increasing Attack Speed each time he successfully hits a spell, stacking up to 5 times.

Q - Mystic Shot: Ezreal fires a damaging bolt of energy which reduces all of his cooldowns slightly if it strikes an enemy unit.

W - Essence Flux: Ezreal fires a fluctuating wave of energy, decreasing the Attack Speed and dealing magic damage to enemy champions, while healing and increasing the Attack Speed of allied champions.

E - Arcane Shift: Ezreal teleports to a target nearby location and fires a homing bolt which strikes the nearest enemy unit.

R - Trueshot Barrage: Ezreal channels for 1 second to fire a powerful barrage of energy missiles which do massive damage to each unit they pass through (deals 10% less damage to each unit it passes through).
