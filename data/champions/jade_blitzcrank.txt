Blitzcrank, the The Great Steam Golem

- Static Field (R) passive zaps nearby enemies

Role: Tank, Support
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Mana Barrier: When Blitzcrank life is brought below 20% health they activate Mana Barrier. This creates a mana shield equal to 50% of their mana for 10 seconds. Mana Barrier can only occur once every 90 seconds.

Q - Rocket Grab: Blitzcrank fires their right hand to grab an opponent on its path, dealing damage and dragging it back to them.

W - Overdrive: Blitzcrank supercharges themself to get dramatically increased movement and attack speed.

E - Power Fist: Blitzcrank charges up their fist to make their next attack deal double damage and pop their target up in the air.

R - Static Field: Passively causes lightning bolts to damage a nearby enemy. Additionally, Blitzcrank can activate this ability to damage nearby enemies and silence them for 0.5 seconds, but doing so removes the passive lightning until Static Field becomes available again.
