Heimerdinger, the The Revered Inventor

- The abilities may look similar but are very different - UPGRADE!!! (R) is a buff that lasts a set time not a single ability - H-28G Evolution Turrets (Q) are active globally but die when Heimerdinger does

Role: Mage, Support
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Techmaturgical Repair Bots: Heimerdinger gives nearby allied Turrets and Champions increased Health Regeneration.

Q - H-28G Evolution Turret: Heimerdinger lays down a machine gun turret. Turrets gain abilities as this skill is increased. (Turrets deal half damage to towers).

W - Hextech Micro-Rockets: Heimerdinger fires long-range rockets that hit the enemies closest to Heimerdinger.

E - CH-1 Concussion Grenade: Heimerdinger lobs a grenade at a location, dealing damage to enemy units as well as stunning anyone directly hit and blinding surrounding units.

R - UPGRADE!!!: Passive / Active: Passively increases Cooldown Reduction.  Activate to heal all active Evolution Turrets and cause them to fire frost shots, which reduce Movement Speed, for a short time.
