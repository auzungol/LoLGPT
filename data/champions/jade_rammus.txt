Rammus, the The Armordillo

- Tremors (R) is a repeating AoE around him - All other abilities are ok

Role: Tank
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Spiked Shell: Rammus gains additional damage as his shell becomes reinforced, converting 25% of his armor into Attack Damage.

Q - Powerball: Rammus accelerates in a ball towards his enemies, dealing damage and slowing targets affected by the impact.

W - Defensive Ball Curl: Rammus goes into a defensive formation, vastly increasing his armor and magic resistance, while returning damage to attacks.

E - Puncturing Taunt: Rammus taunts an enemy champion or monster into a reckless assault against Rammus' hard shell, reducing Armor temporarily.

R - Tremors: Rammus creates waves of destruction pulsing through the ground, causing damage to units and structures near him.
