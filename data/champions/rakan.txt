Rakan, the The Charmer

As mercurial as he is charming, Rakan is an infamous vastayan troublemaker and the greatest battle-dancer in Lhotlan tribal history. To the humans of the Ionian highlands, his name has long been synonymous with wild festivals, uncontrollable parties, and anarchic music. Few would suspect this energetic, traveling showman is also partner to the rebel Xayah, and is dedicated to her cause.

Role: Support
Region: Ionia
Resource: Mana

Passive - Fey Feathers: Rakan periodically gains a shield.

Q - Gleaming Quill: Flings a magical feather that deals magic damage. Striking a champion or epic monster enables Rakan to heal his allies.

W - Grand Entrance: Dashes to a location, knocking up nearby enemies on arrival.

E - Battle Dance: Flies to an allied champion granting them a shield. Can be re-cast for free for a short duration.

R - The Quickness: Gains Move Speed, charming and dealing magic damage to enemies touched.
