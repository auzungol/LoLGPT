Ahri, the Nine-Tailed Fox

Ahri is a vastayan mage who can manipulate magic to charm her prey and steal their life essence. Once a fox-like vastaya with a hunger for magical energy, she consumed so much that she began to take a human's shape. Even now, she struggles with the balance between her animal instincts and her growing humanity.

Role: Mage / Assassin
Range: Ranged
Region: Ionia
Resource: Mana
Difficulty: Moderate
