Twitch, the The Plague Rat

- Deliberating Poison (W) is an AoE spell around him - Ambush (Q) can last up to 50 seconds - Expunge (E) removes poison stacks on enemies

Role: Marksman, Assassin
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Deadly Venom: Twitch's basic attacks infect the target, dealing magic damage each second.

Q - Ambush: Twitch turns Invisible for several seconds, gaining Move Speed. He then gains Attack Speed after coming out of Invisibility.

W - Venom Cask: Twitch hurls a cask of venom that explodes, inflicting Deadly Venom and slowing enemies in the area.

E - Expunge: All nearby enemies secrete Twitch's toxic venoms from their bodies, dealing damage for each stack.

R - Spray and Pray: Twitch closes his eyes and fires his crossbow, rapidly spraying powerful piercing bolts ahead of him.
