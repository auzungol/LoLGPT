Gragas, the The Rabble Rouser

- Barrel Roll (Q) does full damage no matter time it is on the ground - Drunken Rage (W) last for a long time

Role: Fighter, Mage
Region: Unknown
Resource: Mana

Passive - Happy Hour: On ability use, Gragas takes a drink, restoring some of his Health.

Q - Barrel Roll: Gragas rolls a barrel that explodes after a short delay, damaging and slowing Attack Speed. Recast to detonate the barrel early.

W - Drunken Rage: Gragas guzzles down brew from his cask, restoring Mana. He then gains Attack Damage and reduces damage taken for several seconds.

E - Body Slam: Gragas charges forward, colliding with the first enemy, damaging nearby enemies and Slowing them.

R - Explosive Cask: Gragas hurls his cask, damaging and Knocking Away enemies from the impact.
